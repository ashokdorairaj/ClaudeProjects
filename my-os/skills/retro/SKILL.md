# Skill: Retrospective

## When to Use
At the end of a project, sprint, or significant milestone.

## Steps
1. Ask: what project/period are we retro-ing on?
2. Gather input across 4 areas (ask user or infer from context):
   - What went well?
   - What didn't go well?
   - What was surprising?
   - What would we do differently?
3. Identify top 3 action items with owners
4. Save output to plans/retrospectives/<date>-<project>.md
5. Update memory/health-markers.md with any invalidated assumptions
6. Append learnings to LEARNINGS.md

## Output Format
- Date, Project
- Went Well
- Didn't Go Well
- Surprises
- Do Differently
- Action Items (item | owner | due date)
