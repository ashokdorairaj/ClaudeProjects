# Skill: Stakeholder Comms

## When to Use
When writing updates, status reports, or messages for stakeholders.

## Steps
1. Ask: who is the audience, what do they care about, what's the context?
2. Check memory/people.md for communication style preferences
3. Draft in the right tone (executive = brief & outcome-focused, engineer = detailed & technical)
4. Structure: TL;DR → Context → Key Updates → Risks/Blockers → Next Steps
5. Append learnings to LEARNINGS.md

## Output Formats
- **Slack update**: 3-5 lines, bullet points, emoji optional
- **Email**: Subject + TL;DR + body + ask
- **Executive summary**: 1 paragraph, outcome-first
- **Status report**: Table with status, progress, blockers, next
